# Sample noseprints for the live app

Two photos of one registered animal's nose (`nose_1.jpg`, `nose_2.jpg`), taken a moment apart, and the same pair with synthetic sun glare (`glare_1.jpg`, `glare_2.jpg`).

Try a verify visit:

1. Open the live app and enter the officer passcode.
2. Choose **Verify**, type the owner **Sibanda**, and begin.
3. Choose **Choose photos instead** and pick `glare_1.jpg` and `glare_2.jpg`. Mombe reads the glare and asks for a retake.
4. Pick `nose_1.jpg` and `nose_2.jpg`. Mombe checks the nose is live, identifies the animal and checks the owner in the register.
5. Tap **See the reasoning** to read the trace.

The photos come from the Beef Cattle Muzzle/Noseprint database for individual identification: Yijie Xiong, Guoming Li, Galen Erickson (2022), Zenodo, doi:10.5281/zenodo.6324361, licensed CC BY 4.0. The glare is added by Mombe's capture simulator.
